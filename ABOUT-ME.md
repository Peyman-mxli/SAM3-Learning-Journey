# 👁️ Why I Am Learning SAM 3

I am learning **Segment Anything Model 3 (SAM 3)** because segmentation is one of the most powerful bridges between visual perception and practical AI systems.

Object detection can tell us that an object exists and approximately where it is located. Segmentation goes further by identifying the pixels belonging to the object.

That makes it possible to ask richer questions such as:

- Exactly which pixels belong to an object?
- How much area does it occupy?
- Can it be isolated from the background?
- Can it be followed across video frames?
- Can detections be filtered before segmentation?
- Can objects entering or leaving a region be counted?
- Can segmentation results become structured analytics?
- Can another AI model reason over those visual measurements?
- Can SAM 3 become a perception tool inside a larger AI agent?

These are the kinds of systems I want to understand and build.

# 🧪 1. Laboratorio de Visión Computacional

The **Laboratorio de Visión Computacional** is a course tool designed to help turn a learner's personal context and interests into a concrete Computer Vision project idea.

You answer a short form about:

- Your background
- Your interests
- The problems you want to explore
- What tools and resources you have available
- What type of Computer Vision project makes sense for you

The tool then proposes a project that is relevant to your context.

For me, this is valuable because I do not want to complete lessons without direction. I want to transform what I learn into projects that demonstrate real Computer Vision skills.

### Where to find it

It is available through the SAM 3 course portal:

https://sam-3-vision-computacional.vercel.app/

The exact location may change as the course platform is updated, so I prefer to reference the course portal instead of documenting an unverified deep link.

# 🧭 2. Test de Diagnóstico Formativo

The **Test de Diagnóstico Formativo** is a learning diagnostic rather than a traditional pass/fail exam.

Its purpose is to help identify:

- Concepts I already understand
- Concepts I partially understand
- Areas where I need more practice
- Topics where I may have answered correctly but with low confidence
- How my knowledge changes over repeated attempts

The diagnostic acts as a learning map.

Instead of studying everything equally, I can use the results to decide what I should strengthen next.
